# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (version 2.1),
in short form.

## Our standard
We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their background, experience, identity or opinions.

**Good:** being kind and patient; giving and accepting feedback gracefully; focusing on what is best for the project and its users; admitting
mistakes and learning from them.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, unwelcome sexual attention, sharing other people's private
information, or sustained disruption of discussions. Promoting scams, pump-and-dump schemes or trading "signals" in this project's spaces
is also not acceptable: this is a read-only data viewer, not a place for financial promotion.

## Scope
This applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement
The maintainer, [@anjavera](https://github.com/anjavera), is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove
comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break them. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
(for example by opening a private security advisory on this repository, or by writing to [@anjavera](https://github.com/anjavera)).
Reports are handled fairly and kept confidential.
